## A.1 Central proposal

The Frequenzgesetz explores whether frequency and phase relations can be regarded as more fundamental than the measured quantities of time, space, and mass.

Its central *hypothesis* is that apparently different particle states may be observable manifestations of one underlying fundamental structure, distinguished by their characteristic dynamical periods or frequency states.

This is **not** the claim that an electron, proton, and neutron have equal measured rest mass. They demonstrably do not. The proposed commonality concerns a hypothesized underlying structure *before* these distinct measurable states arise.

In the author's shorthand: **“Everything is RAM; matter is ROM.”** These are computer-architecture metaphors, not physical definitions.

## A.2 Null Space: no assumed physical resistance

The model's **Null Space** is an ontological starting point: an undifferentiated potential state that does not presuppose familiar three-dimensional physical space, ordinary physical time, or already formed matter.

A further assumption of the model is that **no physical resistance in the ordinary sense is present at this starting level**. This is a *postulate*, not an experimental characterization of a measured vacuum.

Crucially, resistance, friction, inertial mass, and interactions are not interchangeable quantities in established physics. “Resistance-free” in the proposal does not by itself mathematically imply “massless,” nor does it establish a mechanism by which mass subsequently appears.

The question being posed is:

> **How can a stable observable state with a particular rest mass emerge from a hypothesized underlying condition in which ordinary physical resistance is absent?**

The author reports having a nonpublic mathematical/computational proposal addressing this transition. Its internal mechanism is **not disclosed in this appendix** and its predictive capability has not yet been independently established.

## A.3 Conceptual emergence sequence

The proposed explanatory sequence is:

**Potential / Null Space → differentiation → frequency structure → phase relationships → stable resonance state → observable particle properties**

This sequence describes an *ontological order*, not a verified chronological history or experimentally established chain of transitions.

Within the model, a particle is not first imagined as a tiny material object born inside pre-existing classical space. Rather, a stable state is hypothesized to become describable *as* a particle with measurable properties only at the observable physical level.

A quantitative physical theory would need a defined state space, an evolution law, a stability condition, and a mapping to measurable particle properties.

## A.4 Characteristic period and observable rest mass

The relevant **established** relationships are

\[
E = hf, \qquad E_0 = mc^2,
\]

which, for the rest energy and its associated Compton frequency, give

\[
f_C = \frac{mc^2}{h}, \qquad
\tau_C = \frac{1}{f_C}, \qquad
\boxed{m = \frac{h}{c^2\tau_C}}.
\]

Here \(\tau_C\) is the **Compton period**, not automatically a directly observed internal clock. The equations are established relations and algebraic rearrangements: on their own they **do not predict an unknown particle mass**.

The separate, unverified hypothesis of this appendix is that the *allowed characteristic periods themselves* might be derivable from an underlying structure without entering the already-known particle mass.

That is the decisive validation question. A mass obtained by entering a measured mass into \(f_C=mc^2/h\) and then reversing the equation is a consistency check, **not an independent derivation**.

## A.5 Time, mass, resistance, and ROM

The author's conceptual shorthand is:

**Temporal differentiation → mass → resistance → stabilized matter (ROM)**

This arrow sequence is an **interpretive hypothesis**, not an equation of physics.

- **Time / characteristic period:** distinguishable dynamical phase progression within the proposed model.
- **Mass:** the observed rest mass associated with a realized particle state.
- **Resistance:** in this analogy, the proposed link to a stable state resisting changes in motion. This should not be confused with friction or dissipation; in established mechanics, inertia and inertial mass are closely related rather than sequentially proven causes.
- **RAM:** the continuously variable, dynamic potential in the computer metaphor.
- **ROM:** a comparatively stable and recognizable pattern within continuing dynamics, **not** literally read-only, unchanging matter.

Thus **“Everything is RAM; matter is ROM”** means that matter is imagined as a stable configuration of an underlying dynamic process. No literal computer memory or physical storage medium is asserted.

## A.6 Null Space, Hilbert space, and observable space

**Hilbert space** is the mathematical state space used by quantum theory; it is not ordinary three-dimensional spatial geometry.

The model's **Null Space** is an ontological concept and must **not** be identified with the entire Hilbert space, a mathematical null space (kernel), or the quantum vacuum without an additional precise definition and derivation.

The proposal asks whether spatial localization and observable particle properties might arise from a more basic state structure. No such mapping is established here.

## A.7 Zitterbewegung: relevant but not a proof

In the Dirac theory, **Zitterbewegung** describes an oscillatory contribution in certain treatments of relativistic quantum dynamics, conventionally associated with interference between positive- and negative-energy components. It should not simply be portrayed as literal continuous shaking of every particle.

For a free Dirac particle at rest, the characteristic angular frequency commonly quoted is

\[
\omega_Z = \frac{2mc^2}{\hbar},
\]

distinct from the Compton angular frequency \(\omega_C=mc^2/\hbar\) by a factor of two.

These relations motivate a question about characteristic particle dynamics; they **do not independently explain the origin of its mass**. In particular, inserting known \(m\) merely reproduces a known frequency.

## A.8 Separate research direction: coupling as the program

A related but logically **separate** proposal in the CARA-UTM work is:

> **“The coupling is the program.”**

It asks whether, for certain computational tasks, an engineered system of phase/frequency states and physical couplings could realize a computation without relying exclusively on a prescribed sequence of discrete gates.

A proposed **resonance compiler** would translate a logical problem into a physically realizable coupling configuration. Any genuine advantage must be tested against appropriate circuit-based and Hamiltonian/analog baselines, including preparation, calibration, control precision, noise, readout, and fault-tolerance costs.

This computing proposal is **not** a demonstrated consequence of the mass-emergence hypothesis.

## A.9 Protected mechanism and independent testing

The author intends to keep the internal mass-generation calculation and related source code private for now. This appendix deliberately does **not** reveal its algorithm, closure conditions, or implementation.

Source-code confidentiality can coexist with a meaningful test, provided that the claims are narrow and the protocol independently auditable. A possible **black-box validation** would require:

1. A frozen software version and recorded integrity hash before testing.
2. Preregistered inputs, allowed constants/parameters, units, numerical tolerances, and prediction targets.
3. Clear disclosure of which measured masses, fitted parameters, or lookup data (if any) are used, without requiring disclosure of source code.
4. Predictions committed before reference answers are disclosed or before new experimental measurements are available.
5. Independent evaluation against published baselines, including negative results.
6. A clear distinction between predictive success, a claim about physical causation, and mere reproduction of known formulae.

A successful black-box prediction would support a limited performance claim; it would **not, by itself, prove the proposed ontology**.

## A.10 Open questions

- What law selects one stable characteristic period rather than another?
- Can periods and particle masses be predicted **without** using known masses as calibration or input?
- How would spin, charge, quantum numbers, interactions, and bound states arise?
- What is the mathematical map from the proposed fundamental state structure to observable space and standard quantum states?
- What measurable result differs from established physical theories, and what outcome would falsify the proposal?
- Could the separate coupling-based computing proposal be benchmarked on an experimentally realistic platform?

## A.11 Conclusion and scope

**Working hypothesis:** measured differences between particle rest masses might arise from different characteristic time/frequency states of a shared deeper structure. The proposed Null Space is resistance-free by assumption, while stable observable states are described metaphorically as ROM arising within dynamic RAM.

The known mass–frequency–period equations provide mathematical context **but not yet an independent derivation of particle masses or an empirical proof of the mechanism**. The transition mechanism remains protected and unpublished. Future work should concentrate on sharply specified, independently verifiable predictions.

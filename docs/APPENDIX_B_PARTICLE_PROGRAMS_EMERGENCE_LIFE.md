# Appendix B — Particle Programs, Coupling, Emergence, and the Question of Life

**Universal Time Solver / Frequenzgesetz — exploratory research note**  
**Author:** Christian Berrang  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Status:** Conceptual analogy and open research hypotheses; not an established derivation of matter or life

> **Evidence boundary.** “Program,” “source code,” “RAM,” and “ROM” are metaphors for physical states, constraints, interactions, and retained information. No literal software inside particles, conscious intention in a prephysical state, or derivation of life from resonance is claimed. Physical laws governing particles and many-body systems are established in their respective domains; the proposed frequency-first explanation is not.

## B.1 Particles as different programs

The working analogy treats each particle species as a distinct **program**: a physically identifiable class of states characterized by properties and allowed interactions.

The important next step is **not** that one identical program is copied repeatedly. Different kinds of particles have different physical characteristics, and **their connections matter**.

In established physics, properties such as mass, charge, spin, and other quantum numbers constrain interactions through conservation laws and selection rules. The analogy calls these constraints a *program's interface*, without claiming the particle executes literal source code.

Two separate questions must remain visible:

1. Which possibilities are already specified by the participating physical laws and constituent states?
2. Which collective properties appear only when multiple constituents interact and organize into a larger system?

## B.2 Coupling can produce higher-level behavior

A coupled system can display properties that its constituents do not exhibit in isolation: collective modes, correlations, stable configurations, phase coherence, and effective descriptions at a new scale.

The proposal to investigate is:

> **Can elementary interaction constraints, combined with resonance, coupling, and persistent physical records, support emergent levels of organization with their own effective dynamical rules?**

This is different from asserting that new fundamental physical laws are created whenever particles connect. Effective descriptions can emerge from known underlying interactions without adding fundamental forces or changing conservation laws.

In the computational analogy, connecting constituent “programs” may create a larger *functional program* whose behavior depends on the arrangement and interactions, not just on the list of constituents.

This motivates the phrase **“The coupling is the program.”** Here it describes a hypothesis about physical organization; in the separate CARA-UTM computing proposal it describes a potential method for implementing a computation through controlled couplings. The two uses should not be confused or treated as proof of each other.

## B.3 RAM and ROM: dynamics and persistent information

The author's metaphor **“Everything is RAM; matter is ROM”** distinguishes ongoing dynamics from comparatively persistent physical patterns.

- **RAM:** the continuously changing dynamics and possible configurations of the proposed foundational structure.
- **ROM:** sufficiently stable configurations that preserve identifiable differences over a useful interval.
- **Information:** distinctions between physical states that can be encoded, retained, correlated, and potentially read out.

The metaphor does **not** mean that matter is literally read-only or that every stable structure stores usable information indefinitely. Stability depends on the physical environment, interactions, and timescale.

The earlier [Appendix A](APPENDIX_A_MASS_TIME_EMERGENCE.md) presents the related, separate hypothesis about characteristic particle periods, mass, and an undisclosed mechanism. No such mechanism is exposed or inferred here.

**Stable structure and recoverable difference.** In the relational system-time investigation (Appendix C, forthcoming), temporal order is distinguished from rhythm and from distinguishability. A causal or mathematical order may exist as a relation without any system recording it. What ROM-like structures could add is operational: they may allow a temporal difference to be physically stored and later recognized, so that an order becomes readable rather than merely definable. This is a hypothesis about access to order, not a claim that order requires a recorder.

**Thermodynamic reference.** Landauer's principle concerns the minimum thermodynamic cost associated with logically irreversible information erasure under suitable conditions. It does not imply that every write operation incurs the same cost. A record that can be erased or rewritten does not, by its mere existence, select a direction of time. The directedness of physical recording requires further attention to irreversible processes, boundary conditions, or causal structure, as discussed in B.6.

## B.4 Bose–Einstein condensates as a comparison

A Bose–Einstein condensate is an experimentally studied quantum many-body state in which many bosons occupy the same quantum state under appropriate conditions.

Cooling changes thermal motion, occupation distributions, and collective coherence. It does **not** remove the atoms or erase their rest masses, and it does not demonstrate a return to the proposed Null Space.

The useful comparison for this research is narrower: **the constituents remain, while their collective state and coherence can change dramatically.** A Bose–Einstein condensate is not a classical coupled-oscillator system; condensation arises from quantum statistics and, where present, interactions. The analogy to synchronization models is therefore limited: both can exhibit changes in measures of collective order, but these measures need not be physically or mathematically identical.

This suggests a concrete question for simulation and, in principle, experiment: **how do individual phase correlations, collective coherence, and the information accessible about the ensemble change across the condensation transition?** A classical coupled-oscillator toy-model analysis discussed in the system-time research reportedly found that stronger coupling increased its order parameter while shortening a defined phase-pattern distinguishability horizon. The corresponding script and reproducible results are **not currently included in this repository**, so this is a proposed comparison rather than verified evidence here. Whether a comparable trade-off exists in a quantum many-body transition remains open; the classical toy-model observation must not be treated as a result about condensates.

In the “particle program” analogy, constituent identity persists while the *operating state* and correlations change. This is a comparison for studying organization and coherence, **not** a direct reversal of hypothesized particle creation.

## B.5 From stable matter to life: an open question

Living systems do more than remain stable. They typically exhibit sustained organization through exchanges of energy and matter, regulated processes, information-bearing structures, and capacities for reproduction and evolution.

A possible research direction is to ask how known physical interactions enable successive levels:

**Constituent interactions → collective dynamics → persistent records → regulated organization → potentially self-maintaining, evolving systems.**

These arrows are a *research map*, not a demonstrated unique origin-of-life pathway.

A new effective system can possess behavior absent from an isolated component, without implying that its fundamental constituents acquire new fundamental laws. In particular, **neither phase coherence nor resonance nor stable storage alone is sufficient to define life**. A crystal has stable structure and can preserve physical differences, yet is not alive. In the program analogy, the proposed steps separating passive storage from active organization are a *research ladder*, not a definition:

1. **Stable structure (ROM):** differences persist over a useful interval.
2. **Stored information:** differences can be encoded and correlated.
3. **Feedback on the system's own states:** stored information is accessed and influences subsequent dynamics.
4. **Self-maintenance and adaptation:** feedback contributes to preservation or modification of organization under perturbations.
5. **Possible basis for complex living systems:** metabolism, boundary maintenance, heredity, variation, and evolution require their own explicit treatment and are **not** derived here.

Feedback alone does not explain life either. Its role in this map is specific: it marks the conceptual transition from a structure that preserves information to a system that uses retained information to influence itself. **ROM preserves; a feedback system can act on what it preserves.**

The framework therefore poses, but does not answer:

> **Under what conditions can coupled physical systems retain information about their organization while actively maintaining and modifying that organization?**

## B.6 Connection to relational system time

The current system-time investigation separates:

1. **Rhythm:** periodic or recurrent structure.
2. **Distinguishability:** relations between different states or components.
3. **Temporal order:** a physically supported before/after relation.
4. **Duration:** a calibrated measure of ordered change.

Coupling and persistent records may be relevant to distinguishability and temporal order. But a stable memory by itself does not explain why recording is physically directed: irreversible processes, boundary conditions, or causal structure may still be required. The direction must not be inserted silently by a cycle counter, an ordered input list, or the language of “first” and “later.”

This appendix does **not** claim that life creates time, or that a condensate provides a universal system clock. It asks whether interactions and record formation help identify the minimal physical prerequisites for an operational temporal order.

## B.7 Suggested falsifiable research steps

1. Specify a minimal coupled-state model and identify all external time parameters and assumed ordering rules.
2. Define measurable proxies for coherence, record persistence, and state distinguishability.
3. Compare coupled and uncoupled configurations under identical conditions.
4. Distinguish equilibrium or reversible state changes from genuinely irreversible record formation.
5. Test whether any result differs from established many-body physics, open-system dynamics, or information theory; otherwise label it an alternative interpretation.
6. Report negative findings, parameter sensitivity, and model limitations.

## B.8 Scope and conclusion

**Working idea:** different particle types can be compared to distinct programs whose allowed interactions support new collective patterns. Coupled structures may preserve information, and progressively organized dynamics raise a deeper question about the emergence of self-maintaining living systems.

**What is established:** constituent properties constrain interactions, collective quantum states exist, and many-body systems can display emergent effective behavior.

**What remains hypothetical:** treating particles as literal or ontologically fundamental programs; deriving matter, life, or the direction of time from a frequency-first logic; and claiming a universal advantage from resonance-based computation.

**Protected research:** no nonpublic mass-generation source code, hidden closure mechanism, or proprietary implementation is included.

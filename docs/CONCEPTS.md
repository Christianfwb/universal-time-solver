# Public concept guide

This guide accompanies the [catalog](../concepts/frequenzgesetz.public.v1.json).
Its references are relative to the repository root, not to the concepts directory.
See [analytical scope](ANALYTICAL_SCOPE.md) before comparing frameworks.

## RAM and ROM

“Everything is RAM; matter is ROM” is the model's analogy for dynamic processes
and stabilized structure. It does not identify matter with literal computer memory.

## Consciousness

`I = F(f, Δφ, R)` names the consciousness/resonance hypothesis in the public
catalog. The catalog leaves the observable I, parameter R and mapping F unspecified;
it is not an implemented consciousness measurement.

## Humor and play

Phase tension and resolution provide the project's playful analogy for humor
and play. The 42 Hz wink is explicitly a metaphor, not a measured law. 🍯

## Layers and extended sequence

[ONTOLOGY](../ONTOLOGY.md) describes the path to measurable duration. The longer
sequence in the explanation router also names mass and resonance. It is a compact
ontological reading, not an additional numerical routine or an experimentally
established sequence of physical transitions.

## Sources and conventions

- [BIPM: SI defining constants](https://www.bipm.org/en/measurement-units/si-defining-constants): h, c and e have exact SI values.
- [NIST CODATA](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/): measured particle masses depend on the stated data edition; the examples retain CODATA 2018 electron/proton/neutron masses.
- [Einstein Online: Planck time](https://www.einstein-online.info/en/explandict/planck-time/): a natural time scale formed from constants.

The mass conversion uses rest energy and its corresponding Compton frequency;
it is not the rest mass of a photon or the frequency of an arbitrary mechanical oscillator.
For duration use constant frequency and accumulated phase, not just wrapped phase.
